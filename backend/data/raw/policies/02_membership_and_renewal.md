# Eligibility

All residents of Ghana are eligible to register with the NHIS. Membership categories include:
- Informal sector adults (premium-paying)
- SSNIT contributors and pensioners (exempt from premium)
- Persons under 18 years of age
- Pregnant women
- Indigents (as classified by the LEAP programme or NHIA assessment)
- Persons aged 70 years and above
- Persons with mental disorders

# How to register

1. Visit the nearest NHIS district office, accredited registration centre, or use the NHIS Mobile Renewal short code *929# (or the MyNHIS app).
2. Provide a valid Ghana Card. The Ghana Card is the primary identifier for NHIS — your NHIS membership number is linked to your Ghana Card PIN.
3. Pay the applicable premium (if not in an exempt category) plus the processing fee.
4. Receive your membership confirmation. Active membership is reflected immediately on the NHIA system; a physical card may also be issued.

# Renewal

- Membership lapses 12 months after the last activation date.
- Active members can renew through *929# on any mobile network in Ghana, through the MyNHIS mobile app, or in person at any NHIS office.
- Mobile renewal requires the Ghana Card PIN and a valid Mobile Money or other supported payment.
- A grace period applies after the expiry date during which renewal can be done without re-registration. After the grace period, members may be required to re-enrol.
- Renewal becomes effective immediately on successful payment confirmation in most cases. There is no longer a 3-month waiting period for renewals (the waiting period applies to brand-new registrations).

# Waiting period for new members

- New members face a waiting period (commonly 1 to 3 months from activation) before benefits become active. Pregnant women and exempt categories may be processed without a waiting period.

# Replacement of lost cards

- Report a lost card at any NHIS office. With the Ghana Card, members can be re-verified and have their membership reactivated without obtaining a new physical card, since the Ghana Card now serves as the NHIS card.

# Premiums

- Premiums for the informal sector are graduated by income band, set by the NHIA, and updated periodically. Children under 18 whose parents/guardians are members do not pay an additional premium beyond the processing fee.
