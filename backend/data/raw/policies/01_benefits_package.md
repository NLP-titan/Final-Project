# Overview

The National Health Insurance Scheme (NHIS) covers approximately 95% of disease conditions seen in healthcare facilities in Ghana. The benefit package is uniform across all accredited providers and applies to active card-holders.

# What is covered

## Outpatient services
- General consultations and reviews at accredited facilities
- Diagnostic tests including basic laboratory and imaging where clinically indicated
- Prescribed medicines listed on the NHIS Medicines List
- Wound dressings, minor surgical procedures and physiotherapy where indicated

## Inpatient services
- General and specialist admissions at accredited facilities
- Surgical operations covered under the NHIS tariff
- Maternity care: antenatal, normal delivery, assisted delivery, caesarean section, postnatal care, and neonatal care for the first 3 months
- Accommodation in general wards (private wards are not covered)

## Emergency services
- Stabilisation of all life-threatening emergencies regardless of card status, with subsequent reconciliation
- Emergency surgery and emergency caesarean section

## Oral health
- Pain relief, simple extractions, temporary fillings and incision and drainage of dental abscesses

## Eye care
- Refraction, eye examination, treatment of common eye conditions, and cataract surgery at accredited facilities

# What is NOT covered (exclusion list)

- Rehabilitation services for substance abuse beyond initial stabilisation
- Cosmetic surgery and aesthetic dental work
- Heart and brain surgery (except those listed in the tariff)
- Cancer treatment other than cervical and breast cancer (cervical and breast cancer treatment is covered subject to the tariff)
- Organ transplantation
- Dialysis for chronic kidney disease beyond acute episodes
- Assisted reproduction (e.g. IVF)
- Antiretroviral medicines (covered separately by the National AIDS Control Programme)
- Mortuary services
- Drugs not on the NHIS Medicines List
- Diagnostic and treatment services not approved by the NHIA

# How co-payment works

Co-payment by NHIS card-holders at accredited facilities for services and medicines on the benefit package is illegal. Members who are charged unauthorised fees should report to the District NHIS office or the NHIA Call Centre.
