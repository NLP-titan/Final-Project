# Member rights at accredited facilities

As an active NHIS member, you have the right to:
- Be attended to at any NHIS-accredited facility without discrimination on the basis of NHIS status.
- Receive all services covered under the benefit package without unauthorised co-payment.
- Receive medicines on the NHIS Medicines List without paying out of pocket, when available at the facility and clinically indicated.
- Request a written explanation if a service or medicine is denied, including whether it is excluded from the benefit package or is unavailable.
- Be referred to a higher level of care when the current facility cannot provide the required service.

# What constitutes a violation

The following are common violations members report and may file complaints about:
- Being charged for services or medicines that are on the benefit package and the NHIS Medicines List.
- Being asked to buy medicines from outside pharmacies when those medicines are on the formulary and the facility has them in stock.
- Being turned away despite holding an active NHIS membership.
- Being told to pay full cash for an emergency before stabilisation.
- Discriminatory queue-jumping for cash-paying patients while NHIS patients wait disproportionately.

# How to file a complaint

1. Speak first to the facility's NHIS desk or in-charge — many issues can be resolved on the spot.
2. If unresolved, file a written complaint at the nearest NHIS District Office. You will be given a complaint reference number.
3. Escalate to the Regional NHIA Office if the District Office cannot resolve within the published timelines.
4. Call the NHIA Call Centre (toll-free) to log a complaint by phone. Have your membership number, the facility name, and the date and details of the incident ready.
5. Use the official NHIS complaints email or web form on the NHIA website if available.

# Timelines

- Acknowledgement of complaint: within 3 working days.
- Initial response and proposed resolution: within 14 working days for routine complaints.
- Complex disputes (e.g. clinical disagreements) may take longer and may be referred to the NHIA's Quality Assurance unit.

# What you should keep

- Your NHIS membership confirmation or Ghana Card.
- Receipts for any payments you were asked to make at the facility (these are essential evidence if you were charged for covered services).
- Names of staff and the date and time of the incident.
- Any prescription you were given.
